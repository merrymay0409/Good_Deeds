GOOD DEEDS — the possessive case
================================

Open "Good Deeds.html" in any browser (double-click it).

Keep the folder together: the HTML file needs the "assets" folder
beside it.

  Good Deeds.html
  assets/feather-1.png     your horizontal feather, cut out
  assets/feather-2.png     your vertical feather, cut out
  assets/chime.mp3         your star sound
  assets/angel-1.png       angel picture 1, cut out
  assets/angel-2.png       angel picture 2, cut out
  assets/angel-alive.webp  the angel video, cut out frame by frame
  assets/angel-alive-phone.webp  a lighter copy of it for phones
  assets/angel-3.png       the happy angel with the dove, kept
                           as a spare in case the video will not load
  assets/angel-voice.m4a   the video's sound

HOW IT PLAYS
- 12 sentences, each with one gap. The cue words sit under the
  sentence in large italics.
- Sentences can be done in any order.
- One attempt per sentence. Pressing "Do this deed" (or Enter)
  records the answer permanently — it cannot be changed afterwards.
- Correct: the chime plays and a feather drifts down. The two
  feathers take turns — horizontal first, then vertical.
- Incorrect: no sound, no feather. The answer is simply recorded.

THE ENDING
When the last gap is filled, the angel appears out of the light —
small and faint at first, growing until she is clear and vivid,
then evaporating again. She is chosen by the score:

  0–6 correct    picture 1
  7–10 correct   picture 2
  11–12 correct  the moving angel, full screen, with the video's sound
                 (always the video here — never a picture, on any screen)

All three are fully cut out — the white paper is gone from the
pictures and from every frame of the video, so only the angel herself
fades in and out.

Once she has gone, one sentence remains on the screen with a button
to play again.

ACCEPTED ANSWERS
Capital letters, extra spaces, curly or straight apostrophes and a
repeated "the" are all forgiven. Apostrophe placement is not — that
is the point of the exercise. Both "neighbours'" and "neighbors'"
count.

SOUND
Browsers block audio until the visitor has clicked on the page.
Since players click a button for every sentence, the chime and the
angel's sound both work normally. If a browser still holds her sound
back, a "Turn on the sound" button appears below her.
